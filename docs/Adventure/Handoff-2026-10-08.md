## The big changes

1. **Working title: Bellwarden: Planes of Nothing.** (Avoid NeoForge, "___ of Eternity", Death Watch/Deathwatch
   in the title. Canon name "Death Watch" is fine inside the game.)
2. **Lore corrections:** "Deathwatch" → "Death Watch". The Death Watch are **Archivists** first (memory is the
   counterattack). Minions of the Nothing are people with a soul cut out, not mindless. Prestige is
   **reincarnation** via the Between. Firstborn ordering added; the Fallen are the last three.
3. **New lore with gameplay weight:** the **Harmonic Bell** (limited-range field that sends souls home before the
   Nothing can unmake them; anchored by Engineers at ritual sites), the **contingent** (why the Initiate works
   alone), the **Archivist** mega AI.
4. **The core loop (most important):** befriend townsfolk → archive their culture → that knowledge earns their
   town a **Bell** → the Nothing still eats the land, but not the people. Seasons = Standard rotation.
5. **Hall of Remembrance:** the Archive, relationship screen and fast travel are one place. Pocket-dimension hall
   per player; door per plane, alcove per town. Quick-menu fast travel from the fortress, or walk a town's
   **echo**. Lost towns: alcove sealed, no fast travel, echo stays walkable. Build note: an echo = the town's real
   map + ghostly filter + people snapshot. Guest visits to a partner's Hall are nice-to-have **only if clean and
   stable — your call.**
6. **Friendship through Magic:** every townsperson has a deck that says who they are; casual games are the heart
   of friendship. Top reward: their **signature card** (also shown in their alcove).
7. **Living towns:** homes, church/temple, town hall, tavern, school, graveyard — every non-shop building feeds
   the Archive. Shops restock by **shipment**; every town has a **general store**.
8. **Characters:** the **Supervisor** (gruff because he cares; scripted "slips" with draft lines for solo and
   co-op), the **Watcher** (starts literal and alien, gradually talks like someone who knows you).
